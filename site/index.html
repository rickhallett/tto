A few things to know.

What gets turned off?

Chat apps and websites such as ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini. Coding assistants such as Codex, Cursor and Copilot. Local models through Ollama and LM Studio. Choose the categories you want to block.

What stays untouched?

Your files, email, ordinary browsing and the rest of your Mac. Your code editor stays open; its AI goes quiet.

Can I end a block early?

There’s no in-app override. For emergencies, there’s a deliberate manual recovery procedure. See the documented limits of the block.

Does it need an account?

No account, no subscription. Install the app and authorise its helper once. The app runs locally and does not collect personal data. The source is public.
