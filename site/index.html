Turn Them Off.

A small Mac app that turns off every AI you can reach, until a time you chose, with no way to change your mind early.

99p, once. No subscription. macOS 12 or later.

What it does

You pick what to turn off, and until when. Tonight. The weekend. Then it is off: the chat apps and the websites, the AI in your code editor, the models on your Mac. All of them, at once.

At the time you chose, everything comes back on its own. You do not have to do anything. That is rather the point.

What gets turned off

  • Chat apps and websitesChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Perplexity, Grok, the character bots. The app and the website.
  • AI in your code editor and terminalClaude Code, Codex, Cursor, Copilot, Gemini. Your editor keeps working. The AI in it goes quiet.
  • AI running on your MacOllama, LM Studio and friends. No internet needed, so we stop the programs themselves.

What does not get touched

Your files. Your editor, and anything unsaved in it. Email, Drive, the rest of the internet, the rest of your Mac. We close the exact doors, not the building.

Why you cannot turn it off early

Because at 2am you will want to, and at 10pm you asked us not to let you.

There is no button. Restarting does nothing. There is a recovery procedure, and it takes about ten minutes on purpose. Most people get halfway through it and go to bed.

Nothing personal

The AIs are very good. That is rather the problem. This is not a productivity tool. It is a sleep tool.

How it works, briefly

You install it, type your password once, and never see anything technical again. A small helper keeps the block in place and takes it away when the time is up. It does not phone home, collect anything, or need an account. The code is public, if you or someone you trust would like to check.

Made for people who noticed.
